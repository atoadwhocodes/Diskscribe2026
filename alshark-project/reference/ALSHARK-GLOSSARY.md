# ALSHARK TRANSLATION GLOSSARY
## Official Names & Terminology from the Game Manual and Character Guide

---

## MAIN PARTY CHARACTERS

| Japanese | English | Notes |
|----------|---------|-------|
| シオン・アスマーン | Sion Asmarn | Martian, male, 18. Protagonist. Settler's son dreaming of captaining a ship. |
| ショーコ・ペンローズ | Shoko Penrose | Martian, female, 18. Sion's childhood friend/girlfriend. Genius physicist's daughter. Strongest psychic. |
| カル | Kal | Robot, model K-П 01845, age 33. Obsolete learning robot. Sion's tutor and mascot. |
| スクラップ・ジョー | Scrap Joe | Martian, male, 48. Real name: Joe Compson. Genius mechanic. Builds weapons from scrap. |
| ウェルダ・マレッツ | Werda Mullets | Martian-Zolius hybrid, female, 25. Mutant warrior with supercomputer implant. 225cm tall. |
| ルシア・アスマーン | Lucia Asmarn | Martian, female, 39. Sion's mother. Gains minor psychic abilities. |
| デューク・ギーデル | Duke Giedel | Unknown race, male. Masked knight of Ulyria Kingdom. Raysword master. Golden mask, red hair. |

## ANTAGONISTS

| Japanese | English | Notes |
|----------|---------|-------|
| ジャグマ・ドリアン | Jagma Dorian | Zolius, male, 62. Supreme Commander of Zolius Empire. Two prominent horns = psychic powers. |
| ララウェル・ドリアン | Lalawel Dorian | Zolius, female, 82. Jagma's sister. Commander of space fortress Barbas. Founded Santhana Church. Nickname: "Ice Lalawel". |
| ノヴェル・サリヴァン | Novel Sullivan | Zolius, male, 56. Space Force Supreme Commander. Jagma's cousin. Title: "Sky-Victory Flag Sullivan". Commands battleship Russelgaia. |
| ガドモス・ベイデン | Gadmos Bayden | Zolius, male, 94. Star Force Supreme Commander. 627cm tall, 1580kg. Biosoldier. Title: "Beast Bayden". Created "Black Beast" unit. |
