# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in DiskScribe2026 respectful and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in discussions and reviews.
- Assume good intent and focus feedback on code and outcomes.
- Share context and evidence when reporting issues.
- Respect different viewpoints and experience levels.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, hate speech, threats, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or bad-faith engagement.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Any conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues to `danr@atoadwhocodes.com`.
Include links, screenshots, and timestamps when possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or edit content, limit interaction, or ban contributors who violate this policy.
Enforcement decisions are made to protect the community and project health.
