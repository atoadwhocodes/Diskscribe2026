### How It Works

**Before:**
```
Japanese:        "そうか。わかったよ。" (11 chars)
Translation:     "I see. I understand." (20 chars)
Problem:         Won't fit in 16-char textbox!
```

**After (with Reflow):**
```
Japanese:        "そうか。わかったよ。"
Translation:     "I see. I understand."
Wrapped:         "I see. I\nunderstand."
Result:          2 lines × 16 chars (✓ FITS)
```

### Key Features

**1. Constraint-Based Design**
- Dialog boxes: 16 chars × 3 lines
- Menus: 12 chars × 1 line
- Combat status: 8 chars × 4 lines
- Item names: 10 chars × 1 line
- Profiles per game (Alshark PC-98 included)

**2. Intelligent Breaking**
- English: Breaks at word boundaries (space)
- Japanese: Breaks at character boundaries
- Avoids punctuation at line start (Japanese)
- Hyphenates long words if needed

**3. Status Tracking**
- `ok`: Fits on 1 line
- `wrapped`: Fits across multiple lines
- `truncated`: Forced to fit (aggressive mode)
- `overflow`: Exceeds constraints
- `needs_manual`: Punctuation issues

**4. Real-Time UI Updates**
- Shows wrapped preview instantly
- Status badges (✓, ⚠️, ❌)
- Updates as user edits translations
